# VCSL harmonica samples

Source: [Versilian Community Sample Library](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL),
`Aerophones/Free Aerophones/Harmonica-Hohner-Special20-C/Sustains/Normal`.

License: [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL/blob/master/LICENSE).
The publisher permits use, modification, and redistribution, including commercial use.

The nine `C3.mp3`–`C6.mp3` files are converted from the matching VCSL
`Hohner-Special20_Normal_*.wav` recordings. Conversion: mono 44.1 kHz MP3 at
128 kb/s, with per-note static gain adjustment to approximately -22 dB mean
level. Playback creates a short crossfade within the sustained part for looping.
